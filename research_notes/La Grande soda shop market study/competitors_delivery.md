# Competitive Landscape and Delivery: Quick-Service Food & Drinks in La Grande / Island City, OR (97850)

Method note: Research was conducted 2026-10-08. Direct page fetches to Yelp, DoorDash, Toast, La Grande Observer, Baker City Herald, Restaurantji and Prism News were blocked in this environment (DNS/proxy refusal), so all findings come from search-engine summaries and snippets of those pages. Figures (ratings, hours, fees) are snapshots with uncertain dates and should be re-verified on Google Maps/Yelp before going into a final report.

## 1. Quick-service, fast-casual, coffee and specialty drink businesses in La Grande / Island City

### Takeaway
La Grande's quick-service market is a cluster of national chains along Island Ave near the I-84 Exit 261 interchange (McDonald's, Wendy's, DQ, Subway, Arby's, Taco Bell, Starbucks, Dutch Bros on Q Ave), plus Adams Ave chains and locals (KFC, Burger King, Nells-N-Out, Soda Blitz, The Local, Buckskin). Drive-thru coffee is crowded: Dutch Bros, Starbucks, two Antlers stands, Buckskin, The Local, Sorbenots and Island Espresso. Soda Blitz looks like the only dedicated "dirty soda" shop. Its closest drink-menu rival is Nells-N-Out, which sells cream and mixed sodas plus energy drinks, and Dutch Bros, which sells sodas and energy drinks.

## 3. What makes Antlers / top local coffee shacks succeed

### Takeaway
Antlers' advantages are an early opening (5 AM, with cars waiting at open), two drive-thru locations on commuter routes (Washington Ave and Island Ave toward I-84/Island City), monthly rotating flavors, energy drinks (Zip Fizz), friendly baristas, a local origin story, and app ordering through Joe Coffee. Its main weakness is long lines.

### Cited Findings
- Founded by Justin and Jenna Russell, shed hunters who sold collected antlers to fund the 2011 startup. The Washington Ave stand opened Sept 2011 and the Island Ave stand in 2016. Business at both is "regularly brisk" from about 7 AM until early-evening close, and up to five cars wait when it opens at 5 AM. The business celebrated 10 years in 2021 — [La Grande Observer](https://lagrandeobserver.com/?p=217385); [Baker City Herald](https://bakercityherald.com/?p=80116)
- Menu draws: flavored mochas, monthly flavors, Zip Fizz energy drinks, Huckleberry blended mocha, Spiced White Mocha, Macaroon Mocha — [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/antlers-espresso-la-grande-2); [Restaurant Guru](https://restaurantguru.com/Antlers-La-Grande)
- Listed on Joe Coffee (an order-ahead and loyalty app), where it has 213 reviews at 4.7 — [Joe Coffee](https://joe.coffee/locations/or/la-grande/antlers-espresso-la-grande/)
- Gift cards sold via Giftly — [Giftly](https://www.giftly.com/gift-card/the-local-no-title)

### Inferences
- Antlers wins the early-morning commuter slot (5 to 9 AM), which Soda Blitz, opening at about 10 AM, does not contest. Energy drinks (Zip Fizz, Rebel at Dutch Bros) are the overlap where morning coffee customers could also become soda customers.
- Order-ahead (Joe Coffee) and rotating monthly specials build habit and help offset lines. Soda Blitz could copy both (monthly featured soda, punch card or app).

### Gaps
- Antlers prices and any loyalty-card details were not found. No news coverage after 2021 was found.

## 5. Gaps in the local market

### Takeaway

Chains cover late night (Wendy's to midnight, Arby's to 11 PM, Dutch Bros to 10 or 11 PM) and breakfast (McDonald's, Wendy's, coffee stands from 5 AM).

### Cited Findings
- Late-night coverage: Wendy's drive-thru until 12 AM, Arby's until 11 PM, Dutch Bros until 10 to 11 PM, KFC and Subway until 10 PM — [Wendy's](https://locations.wendys.com/united-states/or/la-grande/2304-island-ave); [Arby's](https://www.arbys.com/locations/us/or/la-grande/2602-island-ave/store-9102/); [Dutch Bros](https://locations.dutchbros.com/dutch-bros-coffee-06f30045919f); [KFC](https://locations.kfc.com/or/la-grande/1706-adams-avenue)

## 6. Delivery: platforms, coverage and merchant pricing

### Takeaway
DoorDash is clearly active in La Grande and carries chains plus some locals, with $0-fee promos on some listings. Uber Eats also has a La Grande city page, recruits couriers there, and lists Starbucks and Pizza Hut. Grubhub presence is unconfirmed, and no independent local delivery service was found. Merchant commissions in 2026: DoorDash charges 15%, 25% or 30% (Basic, Plus, Premier), plus 6% on pickup. Uber Eats charges 20%, 25% (30% on Uber One orders) or 30% (Lite, Plus, Premium), plus 7% on pickup. DoorDash Storefront is commission-free (2.9% + $0.30 card processing), and Drive On-Demand costs a flat $6.99 to $10.99 per delivery.

### Cited Findings
- DoorDash has La Grande category pages (fast food, cafe, subs, restaurants). Listings include McDonald's, Taco Bell, Taco Time, DQ, Subway, Arby's and Cinco de Mayo, several with $0 delivery fees and 17 to 25 minute estimates — [DoorDash fast food](https://www.doordash.com/en/food-delivery/la-grande-or-restaurants/fast-food/); [DoorDash cafe](https://www.doordash.com/food-delivery/la-grande-or-restaurants/cafe/); [DoorDash La Grande](https://www.doordash.com/food-delivery/la-grande-or-restaurants/)

### Inferences
- For drinks with ice (melt, spill risk) and low tickets ($5 to $8), a 25 to 30% commission is punishing. DoorDash Basic (15%) with menu prices marked up about 15 to 20% is the cheapest way to be listed. Storefront plus pickup costs about 3% and fits the drive-thru model best.
- Soda Blitz does not appear in any DoorDash or Uber Eats search results, so it is probably not listed. That leaves an open slot in the "dessert/drinks delivery" category.

- Whether Soda Blitz, Dutch Bros, Antlers or Nells-N-Out are on DoorDash or Uber Eats could not be verified because the store pages could not be fetched.
