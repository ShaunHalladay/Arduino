# Traffic Volumes, Travel Patterns, Tourism and Seasonal Visitor Flows: La Grande, Oregon

Research note (compiled 2026-10-08): oregon.gov, industry.traveloregon.com and several news sites could not be reached from this research environment (DNS/proxy failures on fetch and curl). The findings below come from search-result snippets of primary sources. The **specific ODOT AADT numbers could not be pulled** and need to be looked up by hand (see Gaps). No traffic numbers have been made up.

## 1. ODOT AADT counts: I-84, OR-82 (Island Ave), US-30 (Adams Ave), and truck share

### Takeaway
ODOT publishes AADT by milepoint in its annual Transportation Volume Tables (TVT) and its OTMS/TransGIS tool. La Grande segments appear under the "Old Oregon Trail Hwy No. 6" (I-84), "La Grande-Baker" (US-30) and "Wallowa Lake Hwy No. 10" (OR-82) headings. I could not get the exact figures. I-84 near La Grande is a low-volume, very truck-heavy rural interstate: historically about 43% trucks.

### Cited Findings
- ODOT's Transportation Volume Tables give AADT for each state highway by milepoint, as all-vehicle two-way totals. AADT is the year's total traffic divided by 365. Each year ODOT counts about one third of state highways and adjusts the counts to AADT. The 2022 edition includes a "La Grande-Baker" heading. — [ODOT TVT 2022 (PDF)](https://www.oregon.gov/odot/Data/Documents/TVT_Complete_2022.pdf)
- ODOT's Oregon Traffic Monitoring System (OTMS) is an online interactive tool that gives AADT and truck percentages for each count station. — [ODOT Traffic Counting](https://www.oregon.gov/odot/data/pages/traffic-counting.aspx)
- Context: the least-traveled section of I-84 in Oregon, south of Baker City (about 40 miles southeast of La Grande), carries about 9,400 vehicles per day. Near Portland, I-84 carries over 177,000. — [Wikipedia: I-84 in Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_84_in_Oregon)
- **Truck share:** In 2013 the La Grande Observer reported that trucks were **about 43% of traffic** on I-84 through the Blue Mountains/La Grande area, "a much higher percentage than for other sections of interstate highway in Oregon." (2013 figure; may be out of date.) — [La Grande Observer, "Taming I-84" (2013)](https://lagrandeobserver.com/2013/02/22/taming-i-84/)
- Cabbage Hill (west of La Grande, near Pendleton) is about 7 miles long with an average grade of about 6%. Loaded trucks may climb at less than half the speed limit. Oregon requires trucks to carry chains on the Blue Mountains section of I-84 from Nov 1 to Apr 1. — [Oregon road conditions/truck permit guide](https://www.oregontruckpermit.com/oregon-road-conditions/)
- OR-82 (Wallowa Lake Hwy) runs about 70 miles from I-84/La Grande through Island City, Imbler, Elgin, Wallowa and Enterprise to Joseph/Wallowa Lake. It "sees extra tourist traffic in the summer, mostly from travelers heading to Wallowa Lake." — [Wikipedia: Oregon Route 82](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon_Route_82)
- A La Grande/Island City Transportation System Plan exists (ODOT, document dates 1999–2012). It probably contains city-street ADT tables. — [Oregon Digital Collections: La Grande/Island City TSP](https://digitalcollections.library.oregon.gov/nodes/view/84120)

### Inferences
- I-84 at La Grande probably carries AADT in the low-to-mid teens of thousands. It sits on the same rural through-corridor as the 9,400-vehicle Baker City segment, with La Grande local trips added. This is an estimate and must be checked against the TVT.
- With roughly 40%+ trucks, a large share of I-84 traffic is commercial drivers. They are a potential coffee/energy-drink customer base, but they need truck-accessible parking and stacking. Most semis cannot use a standard drive-thru lane.
- Island Ave (OR-82) between I-84 exit 261 and Island City is probably La Grande's busiest arterial. It carries local commercial traffic plus all Wallowa County–bound tourist traffic.

### Gaps
- **Exact AADT for I-84 at exits 259/261/265, OR-82/Island Ave, US-30/Adams Ave, and recent trends (2019–2024): not obtained.** oregon.gov was unreachable. To fill this gap, open TVT_Complete_2023 or 2024 (oregon.gov/odot/Data/Documents/) or ODOT TransGIS (gis.odot.state.or.us/transgis), and look up Hwy 006 MP ~258–266, Hwy 010 MP 0–3, and Hwy 066 (La Grande-Baker) in town.
- No current (post-2013) truck share figure. ODOT vehicle-class data in OTMS has it.

## 2. Seasonality and winter I-84 closures that strand travelers in La Grande

### Takeaway
I-84 between Pendleton and La Grande (MP 216–265, Cabbage Hill/Blue Mountains) closes several times every winter for snow, ice and spin-outs. Closures happen in other seasons too, after truck crashes. During long closures, stranded trucks fill La Grande's truck stops and highway shoulders, and motels can sell out. ODOT has had to hold westbound trucks back at Ontario because La Grande and Baker City had no parking left. This creates short demand spikes, captive and unplanned, for food and drinks near the I-84 exits.

### Cited Findings
- ODOT's TripCheck closure template for westbound I-84: "truck traffic stranded in La Grande and Baker City have filled the truck stops and adjacent highway shoulders" and "there is no room for trucks to park in Baker City or La Grande." Car travelers are told to confirm motel rooms before heading to La Grande because "during extended closures lodging may be booked full." — [TripCheck: Closed I-84 Westbound](https://tripcheck.com/maps/R5/I84_WB_MP374_216_cars_only.htm)
- November 2022: winter weather closed I-84 between Pendleton and La Grande. ODOT closed westbound I-84 to commercial trucks at Ontario (Exit 374) because Baker City and La Grande had limited truck parking. — [KPTV, Nov 28 2022](https://www.kptv.com/2022/11/28/winter-weather-conditions-close-i-84-between-pendleton-la-grande/)
- January 2022: severe weather and high winds closed I-84. The Observer reported that stuck motorists "could be stranded for hours — and possibly days." — [La Grande Observer, Jan 3 2022](https://lagrandeobserver.com/2022/01/03/severe-weather-and-high-winds-blanket-county-close-i-84/)
- December 1, 2023: spun-out vehicles at MP 223 (top of Cabbage Hill) stopped traffic. March 1, 2024: multiple crashes near MP 222 closed the highway for several hours. — [Elkhorn Media Group, Mar 1 2024](https://elkhornmediagroup.com/?p=176499); [Elkhorn Media Group](https://elkhornmediagroup.com/?p=168450)
- December 2024: I-84 closed between Pendleton and La Grande after several semis spun out. It reopened the next evening. — [Apple Valley News Now](https://www.applevalleynewsnow.com/news/interstate-84-closed-in-oregon-due-to-multi-vehicle-pile-up/article_d8e29b08-6887-4d80-b21b-4d9b9d813cf5.html)
- February 2025: both directions closed MP 216–265 for weather and traction, lifted the same day. — [KGW](https://www.kgw.com/article/traffic/i-84-shut-down-pendleton-la-grande-crashes-ice/283-b4d2c7d8-b7ab-445b-bccf-185db086797a)
- April 10, 2025: a truck crash at MP 222 closed westbound I-84 between La Grande and Pendleton with no reopening estimate. OR-204 (Tollgate) was closed to through traffic. — [East Oregonian](https://eastoregonian.com/?p=685084)
- May 4, 2026: eastbound I-84 closed MP 216–265 between La Grande and Pendleton after a crash. A local detour was set up. — [La Grande Observer, May 4 2026](https://lagrandeobserver.com/2026/05/04/i-84-eastbound-shut-between-la-grande-and-pendleton/)
- June 24, 2026: westbound I-84 closed MP 265–224 (La Grande to Cabbage Hill) after a semi overturned. — [KPTV, June 24 2026](https://www.kptv.com/2026/06/24/overturned-semi-truck-blocks-traffic-i-84-near-la-grande/)
- October/November 2021: a westbound closure of about 4 hours (crash plus fog over the Blues) backed traffic up along Island Avenue. — [La Grande Observer](https://www.lagrandeobserver.com/news/local/i-84-westbound-reopens-after-major-traffic-backup/article_e47bd6ec-3cc5-11ec-9d57-373daf908b65.html)
- Seasonal tourism pattern on OR-82/Wallowa: July and August are the busiest months. Wallowa Lake State Park "is often sold-out in July." After school starts in fall, visitors "drop to a trickle." Joseph sees a second bump during fall hunting season. — [Oregon State Parks: Wallowa Lake](https://stateparks.oregon.gov/index.cfm?do=park.profile&parkId=20); [Wallowa Lake Tourism Guide](https://www.wallowalake.net/about-wallowa-lake); [Wallowa County Chamber](https://www.wallowacountychamber.com/wallowa-lake/)

### Inferences
- Seasonality probably follows a summer peak (June–August: stock show, county fair, Wallowa Lake traffic), a fall shoulder (EOU in session, elk/deer hunting, film festival), and a winter low in leisure traffic. Winter also brings unpredictable closure surges, when stranded drivers jam the exit 261/265 area.
- A location next to I-84 exit 261 (Island Ave) or exit 265 (the truck stops) gains the most during closures.

### Gaps
- No ODOT monthly/seasonal factors (summer vs. winter percent) for La Grande count stations. ODOT ATR (automatic traffic recorder) data in OTMS has them.
- No count of closures per winter or total closure hours. ODOT Region 5 may have a log.

## 3. Main commercial / drive-thru corridors and where fast-food chains cluster

### Takeaway
La Grande's highway-commercial strip is Island Avenue (OR-82), running from I-84 exit 261 east toward Island City. It has the town's only McDonald's. A Dutch Bros already operates at 2003 Q Ave, near that corridor. Adams Ave (US-30) is the downtown main street.

### Cited Findings
- McDonald's at 2308 Island Ave, La Grande, the only McDonald's in La Grande, open 6 AM–11 PM. — [McDonald's locator](https://www.mcdonalds.com/us/en-us/location/or/la-grande/2308-island-ave/5171.html); [Uber Eats](https://www.ubereats.com/store/mcdonalds-2308-island-ave/3hknGD8kXViDUzEMII1-pQ); [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/mcdonalds-la-grande)
- Dutch Bros Coffee, 2003 Q Ave, La Grande. — [Nextdoor listing](https://nextdoor.com/pages/dutch-bros-coffee-la-grande-or/photos/)
- ODOT has a maintenance salt shed on Island Avenue. Island Ave backs up during I-84 closures. — [La Grande Observer 2017](https://lagrandeobserver.com/2017/11/04/salt-will-be-used-on-interstate-84-this-winter/); [La Grande Observer 2021](https://www.lagrandeobserver.com/news/local/i-84-westbound-reopens-after-major-traffic-backup/article_e47bd6ec-3cc5-11ec-9d57-373daf908b65.html)
- OR-82 links I-84 and US-30 and is the region's transportation hub route. — [Wikipedia: OR-82](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon_Route_82)

### Inferences
- The Island Ave stretch from the exit 261 interchange to Island City is probably the main drive-thru cluster (McDonald's, nearby Dutch Bros, and others likely). A competitor-level inventory should come from the parallel competition research.

### Gaps
- Full list of fast-food and coffee chains by corridor not confirmed here (Starbucks, Taco Bell, Burger King, Wendy's, Arby's, Subway, etc.).
- Exit numbering (259 = US-30 west end, 261 = OR-82/Island Ave, 265 = OR-203/US-30 east/truck stops) is from general knowledge and was not verified in sources.

## 4. Tourism: visitor spending for Union County and the Wallowa corridor

### Takeaway
The current Dean Runyan/Travel Oregon report covers calendar 2025 (released April/May 2026). It has Union and Wallowa County tables, but I could not read them. The Eastern Oregon region (seven counties, Union and Wallowa included) had **$549.5 million** in travel spending in 2025. That was the **fastest growth of any Oregon region (+5.4%)**. Statewide spending was $14.6 billion.

### Cited Findings
- Eastern Oregon region travel spending: **$549.5M in 2025**. Umatilla County alone accounted for $250.6M. Eastern Oregon had the highest year-over-year travel-spend growth of the 7 regions, at **+5.4%**. — [Hermiston Herald, May 7 2026](https://hermistonherald.com/2026/05/07/new-report-shows-economic-impact-of-tourism-in-state-region/)
- Oregon statewide: **$14.6B in 2025** (+1.3% nominal over 2024's $14.4B), still about 4.5% below 2019 after inflation. 122,900 direct travel jobs. — [Travel Oregon / Dean Runyan, Economic Impact of Travel CY2025 (PDF)](https://industry.traveloregon.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Travel-Impacts-2025-Oregon_2026_04_09.pdf); [Daily Tidings](https://www.dailytidings.com/oregon-tourism-brings-in-14-6-billion-but-growth-lags-behind-national-pace/)
- Statewide 2024: $14.3B (record at the time, +1.1%). — [Travel Oregon newsroom](https://industry.traveloregon.com/newsroom/news/travel-oregon-marks-national-travel-tourism-week-by-showcasing-community-investments-and-14-3-billion-in-visitor-spending/)
- The report covers all 36 counties for 2003–2025. — [Travel Oregon research page](https://industry.traveloregon.com/research/oregon-travel-impacts/); [Oregon Digital Collections copy](https://digitalcollections.library.oregon.gov/nodes/view/321078)
- Wallowa Lake State Park has 200+ tent/RV sites plus yurts and is one of Oregon's most popular state parks. Sites can be booked 6 months ahead and often sell out in July. — [Oregon State Parks](https://stateparks.oregon.gov/index.cfm?do=park.profile&parkId=20); [Wallowa Lake Tourism Guide](https://www.wallowalake.net/about-wallowa-lake)
- Joseph has about 1,000 residents but draws visitors "especially during the summer and the fall hunting season." — [Wallowa Lake Tourism Guide](https://wallowalake.net/index.php/wallowa-lake-about)
- Travel Oregon promotes La Grande weekend trips and an OR-82 "Union, Wallowa and Lostine" road trip. — [Travel Oregon: Grand weekend in La Grande](https://traveloregon.com/things-to-do/trip-ideas/favorite-trips/grand-weekend-la-grande/); [Travel Oregon road trip](https://traveloregon.com/things-to-do/culture-history/historic-sites-oregon-trail/road-trip-union-wallowa-and-lostine/)

### Inferences
- Union County's share of the $549.5M Eastern Oregon total is probably a modest fraction, a few tens of millions of dollars. Umatilla accounts for about 46%, and the remainder is split among Baker, Union, Wallowa, Malheur, Grant, Harney and Morrow. Check the county table in the CY2025 PDF.
- Essentially all car traffic to Wallowa Lake/Joseph from I-84 runs through La Grande on Island Ave/OR-82. A drive-thru on that route catches summer visitors on both the trip out and the trip back.

### Gaps
- Union County and Wallowa County dollar figures (spending, jobs, local tax, visitor-nights) for 2024/2025 not obtained. They are in the CY2025 Dean Runyan PDF linked above.
- No annual visitor count for Wallowa Lake State Park. OPRD publishes day-use and overnight attendance on request or in annual reports.

## 5. Major annual events and attendance

### Takeaway
The biggest documented draws are the Union County Fair (about 14,000–18,500 attendees over 4 days in early August) and the Eastern Oregon Livestock Show in Union (June, since 1908, roughly 10,000 by an unverified third-party estimate). EOU (about 2,700 students, Fall 2025) gives a steady fall-through-spring base. Fall elk/deer hunting is a recognized but unquantified visitor surge.

### Cited Findings
- **Union County Fair (La Grande, late Jul/early Aug, 4 days):** 2017: 14,947. 2018: 15,354. 2021: about 13,500. 2022 (150th anniversary): 18,000+ (about 18,500), the highest in 6 years. 2023: about 14,000 (down, partly because of weather). 2026 dates: July 29–Aug 1. — [La Grande Observer 2018](https://lagrandeobserver.com/2018/08/06/union-county-fair-continues-growth-after-declines/); [La Grande Observer 2022](https://lagrandeobserver.com/2022/08/10/union-county-fair-draws-more-than-18000-over-four-day-event/); [La Grande Observer 2023](https://www.lagrandeobserver.com/news/local/union-county-fair-sees-decline-in-attendance-numbers-from-previous-year/article_4d34bcf8-3544-11ee-b27e-13e2bc194688.html); [Festivalnet 2026 listing](https://festivalnet.com/31176/La-Grande-Oregon/State-Fairs/Union-County-Fair). Third-party listings give conflicting, unverified totals of 18,000 (2025) and 8,000 (2026). — [cofairs.com](https://cofairs.com/oregon/union-county-fair)
- **Eastern Oregon Livestock Show (Union, OR):** founded 1908, with entries from Union, Baker, Wallowa and Umatilla counties, and a rodeo. 2026 dates: June 10–14. 10times.com's attendance estimate of about 10,000 is unverified. — [La Grande Observer, May 2026](https://lagrandeobserver.com/2026/05/21/stock-show-is-a-june-tradition-in-union/); [OSU Extension](https://extension.oregonstate.edu/4h/events/eastern-oregon-livestock-show); [Rodeoticket](https://www.rodeoticket.com/eastern-oregon-livestock-show/rodeo-information); [10times (estimate)](https://10times.com/eastern-oregon-livestock-show)
- **Eastern Oregon Film Festival:** 2025 edition Oct 16–18. 2026 edition Oct 14–17, with a $75 all-access pass. An older Observer article (8th annual) reported 300 all-access passes plus individual tickets. Sources disagree on the edition number (12th vs. 17th). — [La Grande Observer 2025](https://lagrandeobserver.com/2025/10/04/eastern-oregon-film-festival-returns-oct-16-18-in-la-grande/); [La Grande Observer Sep 2026](https://lagrandeobserver.com/2026/09/26/eastern-oregon-film-festival-returns-oct-14/); [La Grande Observer (older)](https://www.lagrandeobserver.com/news/local/attendance-up-at-annual-film-festival/article_f46dc8f8-c955-5160-a9ba-2e831a3437b6.html)
- **Eastern Oregon University:** Fall 2025 headcount 2,704 (1,849 resident, 855 nonresident), down from 2,798 in Fall 2023. Includes online students. On-campus enrollment rose 7.5% from 2023 to 2025, and graduate enrollment rose about 50%. — [HECC 2025 University Evaluation: EOU (PDF)](https://www.oregon.gov/highered/public-engagement/Documents/Commission/Full-Commission/2026/6-October%207,%208/7.1d%20-%202025%20University%20Evaluation%20-%20Eastern%20Oregon%20University%20(EOU)%20(ORS%20352.061).pdf); [EOU news](https://www.eou.edu/news/eou-sees-more-students-finishing-degrees-plans-for-more-success/); [La Grande Observer, Oct 6 2026](https://lagrandeobserver.com/2026/10/06/eastern-oregon-university-grows-enrollment-graduation-rates/)
- **Mt. Emily Recreation Area (MERA):** 3,669 acres about 2 miles from downtown La Grande, with about 45 miles of motorized and 45 miles of nonmotorized trails. Pay-to-park was approved in Dec 2024 ($5/day, $30/year). It is also winter range for a large elk herd. — [Union County: MERA](https://unioncountyor.gov/public-works/parks/mt-emily-recreation-area/); [East Oregonian, Dec 21 2024](https://eastoregonian.com/2024/12/21/pay-to-park-at-mount-emily-recreation-area-gets-approval/)
- **Anthony Lakes Mountain Resort** (Baker County, about 45 min from La Grande via I-84/North Powder): 7,100 ft base, season Nov–Apr, 21 runs, 1,100 lift-served acres. Historic: about 40,000 visitors in 1969, which the resort says is more than in recent decades. — [Anthony Lakes history](https://anthonylakes.com/company/history/); [Snow-forecast](https://www.snow-forecast.com/resorts/Anthony-Lakes-Mountain-Resort)
- **Hunting:** the fall big-game season is a recognized visitor draw in Union and Wallowa counties. A 2004 Observer article projected about 1,000 hunters in Union County forests for the elk opener (old figure). — [La Grande Observer 2004](https://lagrandeobserver.com/2004/10/22/elk-opener-draws-near/); [La Grande Observer 2023 outlook](https://lagrandeobserver.com/2023/09/22/hankering-for-the-hunt-big-game-outlook-for-the-fall/)

### Inferences
- Event-driven demand peaks: early-to-mid June (stock show), late July/early August (county fair), mid-October (film festival plus hunting season), and the EOU move-in, graduation and athletics weekends.
- Hunters leave early in the morning in October–November, which favors drive-thru coffee that opens early (5 AM).

### Gaps
- "Oregon Trail Days": no source found for an event by that name in La Grande. It may not exist or may go by another name.
- No current attendance for EOU athletics, the stock show, Anthony Lakes skier visits, MERA use, or Grande Ronde river recreation.
- No current ODFW hunter-days figure for Union County units (Starkey, Catherine Creek, Mt. Emily). ODFW harvest statistics have it.

## 6. Lodging counts and transient lodging tax trends

### Takeaway
Union County charges a 3% transient room tax (TRT), and La Grande has its own city TRT. I found no published total collections. The City of La Grande gave the Chamber $103,338 in TRT last year and has proposed cutting that by more than $58,000 for 2026–27. The Chamber reported occupancy demand up 9.5% relative to other Oregon areas. Oregon's state lodging tax rises from 1.5% to 2.75% on Jan 1, 2027.

### Cited Findings
- Union County 3% transient room tax on stays of 30 days or less, distributed as grants. The 2025 revised ordinance (Ord. 2025-03) allocates Tourism Promotion 27%, Events 15%, Discretionary Grants 10%, with other shares to MERA, Buffalo Peak Golf Course and the fairgrounds. — [Union County TRT page](https://unioncountyor.gov/transient-room-tax/); [Ordinance 2025-03 (PDF)](https://unioncountyor.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Ordinance-2025-03-Transient-Room-Tax.pdf)
- City of La Grande gave the Union County Chamber $103,338 in TRT dollars in the past year and proposed a cut of more than $58,000 for 2026–27. The county tourism allocation is estimated at about $91,800. The Chamber director cited occupancy demand up 9.5% compared with other Oregon areas. (AI-generated summary of a public meeting; verify against minutes.) — [CitizenPortal summary](https://citizenportal.ai/articles/9571083/Oregon/Union-County/Union-County-chamber-reports-drop-in-transient-room-tax-support-for-202627)
- State lodging tax: 1.5% through Dec 31, 2026, then 2.75% from Jan 1, 2027. FY2024 statewide collections were $41.5M. — [Oregon DOR Lodging Tax](https://www.oregon.gov/dor/programs/businesses/pages/lodging.aspx); [LRO Report 1-25](https://olis.oregonlegislature.gov/liz/2025R1/Downloads/CommitteeMeetingDocument/304976)
- The Oregon DOR publishes state TLT return statistics by region and county (FY18–FY24), which can be used to back out lodging sales. — [DOR 2025 Edition Lodging Report (PDF)](https://www.oregon.gov/dor/programs/gov-research/Documents/Lodging%20Report%202025%20Edition%20FY18-FY24-Secured.pdf); [data.oregon.gov dataset](https://data.oregon.gov/Administrative/State-Transient-Lodging-Tax-Return-Statistics-by-R/mf7n-egve)

### Inferences
- If $91,800 is 27% of county TRT, total county TRT would be about $340K/year, implying about $11M in taxable room sales at 3%. This depends on how "tourism allocation" is defined, so it is speculative.

### Gaps
- Number of hotels/motels and rooms in La Grande not found. Likely about 10–15 properties clustered at exits 261 and 265, but unverified.
- City and county TRT totals by year, and occupancy and ADR trends, not obtained. They are in city/county budgets and the DOR county tables.
